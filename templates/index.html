      How to Install in Your Browser (Developer Mode)

      The PhishGuardAI browser extension is packaged in the extension/ folder of this project:

      1. Open your browser and navigate to chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions).
      2. Toggle Developer mode in the top-right corner.
      3. Click Load unpacked.
      4. Select the folder: c:\Users\yalla\OneDrive\Desktop\PhishGuardAI\extension.
      5. The PhishGuardAI shield will appear in your toolbar, actively protecting every navigation!
